== 6. Best hypothesis and what testing it costs
H1 (best fit): ana2y2Yv runs two TPU hosts (.112 and .138) and its gossip entry follows the one that is active, with a lag. After a flip the newly listed host receives our unstaked QUIC connection but drops a share of the txs (no pattern by idx, price, arm or gap), while the host that was active before the flip kept delivering 6/6 up to the change. Evidence for: each address delivered 6/6 in the period it was the active one (S1 .112, S3 .138, S4 .138 before 21:58Z) and failed in the period it was not (S2 .138: 0 of 186; S4 .112: 28% lost); the loss is per tx, not per window; RTT differs by 6 ms only (42.1 vs 36.1) and S1/S3 used .112 over the same Wi-Fi without loss. Evidence against or open: S3's gossip change to .138 at 19:10Z was followed by 7 of 7 windows 6/6, i.e. no degraded period after that flip, so "a newly listed host is lossy" is not general; the S4 loss may instead be a property of .112 on that night (load, a connection or rate limit for unstaked senders, a restart).
H2 (alternative): the validator's real leader-time TPU is .138 in S4 as well and .112 is a stale forwarder that relays some txs (the 28% that arrive). This predicts that sending to .138 in S4 would have landed 6/6.
H3 (less likely): our side. Excluded by S1, S3 and the 2 early S4 windows on the same path, and by 0 send errors; not excluded for the one night.

Testing cost:
1. Free, no lamports, no tx: for the next ana2y2Yv windows log the gossip tpuQuic at each prep (getClusterNodes is already called about 200 times a session; the value is only stored on change) and probe both hosts with a QUIC handshake only (connect, no stream). Needs a code change in the sender (crates/, not done here), about a day of work, about 1 credit per probe.
2. A/B delivery test: in one supplementary session send alternate ana2y2Yv windows to .112 and .138, 10 windows each. Cost per window about 31,658 lamports (arm P/C, from the allocation file), so 20 windows is about 0.63 million lamports (1.7% of the 38,000,000 confirmatory budget), about 2 hours of ana2y2Yv leader supply (20.6 windows an hour); it also needs a sender change to send to a named address, and the windows would be marked as a delivery test (ordering_eligible false) and not counted as measurement.
